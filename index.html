6. Protect your profit

  • Set a minimum call-out: consider £40–£50 per visit to help cover travel, setup and equipment.
  • Charge appropriately for one-off and deep cleans: they often take longer than routine maintenance.
  • Ask for photos: especially for driveways, gutters and heavily soiled properties.
  • Be clear about what's included: for example, pressure washing alone versus pressure washing plus re-sanding or waste removal.
  • Allow for difficult access: height, parking, specialist products and extra equipment can add time and risk.
  • Track every job: record the quote, time taken, travel and materials, then review prices after your first 10–20 jobs.
  • Confirm the price before starting: if the scope changes, explain any extra cost and get the customer's agreement first.
